# Recent improvements in Sokker++

Sokker++ now makes player development easier to review directly on Sokker. Hover or focus a first-team player's name on the squad page, player page, training setup, or a training report to open **General Skills ++**. It brings together the player's weekly skill history, a separate chart for each skill, and counts of advanced and formation training since the last observed rise. The weekly report table remains available, with training details, source indicators, and CSV export.

For juniors in your own academy and in training reports, the extension shows a native Canvas chart of observed weekly levels and their trend. The card labels Sokker's talent value separately from an estimate calculated from the observed curve, and it can export the chart as a PNG. Data is stored locally and refreshed on eligible pages as needed; opening a card reads the saved data without making a request on hover.

Both views use the extension's existing design and support its eight interface languages, with English as a fallback. The first-team and junior charts are drawn without a charting library. You can now review skill progress, training history, and junior talent in the Sokker++ extension while browsing the relevant Sokker pages.

See the [feature screenshots](SCREENSHOTS.md). For calculation details and limitations, read the [first-team guide](TALENT_SUMMARY.md) and [junior guide](JUNIOR_TALENT.md).
